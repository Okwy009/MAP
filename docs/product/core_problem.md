The Problem MAP Solves

Most people do not lack goals.

They lack execution under real-life conditions.

Common breakdown points:

Too many possible actions → paralysis
Overplanning → no execution
Emotional overwhelm → avoidance
Inconsistent routines → lost momentum
High cognitive load → burnout

The core failure is not discipline.
It is decision overload at the point of action.

People stop because they don’t know what to do right now in their current state.
