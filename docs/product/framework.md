# MAP Framework

The MAP Framework transforms goals into sustainable daily action.

## Step 1

Define the outcome.

What are you trying to achieve?

---

## Step 2

Assess reality.

How much time and energy do you have today?

---

## Step 3

Reduce complexity.

Break the work into the smallest meaningful action.

---

## Step 4

Execute immediately.

Complete the action before planning anything else.

---

## Step 5

Reflect.

Record progress.

Adjust tomorrow's recommendation.

---

## Core Philosophy

Small actions repeated consistently produce extraordinary outcomes.
