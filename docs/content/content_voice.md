Content Voice & Audience Questionnaire
Part 1: About You (The Writer)
1. Who are you in this content?

I'm a founder building MAP in public and documenting every lesson along the way. I write as a practitioner sharing what I'm learning, not as someone pretending to have all the answers. My goal is to help creators build systems that make consistency inevitable instead of relying on motivation.

2. What's your background with this topic?

I've spent years working with systems, analytics, and process optimization. While building my own writing habit and growing my Substack, I noticed that most creators don't fail because they lack ideas—they fail because they lack a reliable execution system.

That realization led me to build MAP (My Accountability Partner): a product that removes decision fatigue and tells creators exactly what to work on next.

My credibility comes from building the system publicly, testing it on myself, documenting what works, and continuously refining it based on real experience rather than theory.

3. What are 3–5 opinions or beliefs you hold about your industry that not everyone agrees with?
Consistency is a systems problem, not a discipline problem.
Most productivity advice creates more work instead of reducing it.
Creators don't need more ideas—they need fewer decisions.
AI shouldn't replace thinking; it should reduce friction around execution.
Planning is valuable only if it makes today's next action obvious.
