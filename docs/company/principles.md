# MAP Principles

These principles guide every product, design, engineering, and business decision.

## 1. Momentum Beats Motivation

Motivation is temporary.

Momentum is sustainable.

MAP exists to help users continue making progress regardless of how they feel.

---

## 2. Action Over Planning

Planning is valuable only when it leads to execution.

MAP always prioritizes doing over organizing.

---

## 3. Reduce Cognitive Load

Every interaction should reduce mental effort.

If a feature increases decision fatigue, it should be redesigned or removed.

---

## 4. Respect Reality

Users have different amounts of time, energy, and focus each day.

MAP adapts to reality instead of expecting perfect discipline.

---

## 5. One Next Step

MAP should never overwhelm users with long task lists.

The default experience is one clear next action.

---

## 6. Consistency Wins

Missing one day should never feel like failure.

MAP optimizes for long-term consistency rather than perfect streaks.

---

## 7. Evidence Over Assumptions

Product decisions should be based on user behavior whenever possible.

Assumptions should be documented and validated.
