# Ideal Customer

## Primary Customer

Online creators who already believe consistency matters but struggle to maintain it.

Examples include:

- Substack writers
- Independent educators
- Solo founders
- Indie hackers
- Technical writers
- Professionals building a personal brand

---

## Emotional State

They often think:

"I know what I should be doing."

"I just don't keep doing it."

"I keep restarting."

"I overthink everything."

---

## Daily Reality

- Busy job
- Family responsibilities
- Limited energy
- Irregular schedule

---

## Desired Outcome

They want to open MAP and immediately know what to do next without overthinking.
