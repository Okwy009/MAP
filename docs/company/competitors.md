MAP Competitor Landscape

MAP does NOT compete with:

Notion (documentation system)
Todoist (task tracking)
ChatGPT (general reasoning tool)
Habit apps (tracking systems)
MAP competes with:
procrastination
decision fatigue
overthinking
inconsistency
stalled execution
Key Insight

Users don’t switch from tools.

They switch from:

“not doing the work”

to

“doing the next thing”
