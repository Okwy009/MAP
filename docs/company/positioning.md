# MAP Positioning

## Category

MAP is a decision-reduction and accountability system.

---

## Target Customer

Creators and ambitious individuals who struggle to execute consistently because life becomes overwhelming.

---

## Problem

People rarely stop because they lack ideas.

They stop because deciding what to do next becomes mentally exhausting.

---

## Solution

MAP converts goals into one clear next action based on a person's available time, energy, and context.

---

## Differentiation

MAP does not compete by generating better plans.

MAP competes by helping users keep moving.

---

## Positioning Statement

For creators and ambitious individuals who struggle to stay consistent,

MAP is the accountability system that removes overthinking and keeps meaningful work moving forward,

unlike planners, habit trackers, or AI chatbots,

because MAP always knows the most appropriate next step.
